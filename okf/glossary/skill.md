Each capability has exactly one skill at `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`. Skills are prose the
harness reads; they never execute on their own. Each skill pairs with a CLI
[tool](/okf/glossary/tool.md).

Index: [skills index](/okf/skills/index.md).
